# Home Sale Inventory Desk: Friend Demo Brief

## What this does

This turns home-sale photos into a working resale plan:

- identifies sellable items from photos
- separates valuable items from quick-sell items
- creates price bands instead of random guesses
- recommends where each item should be listed
- drafts marketplace posts
- flags items that need model numbers, measurements, labels, or close-ups
- pushes low-value items into bundle, donation, free pickup, recycle, or dispose lanes

## What you send

Send photos in room/category batches:

- Garage
- Kitchen
- Living room
- Bedrooms
- Office
- Patio/outdoor
- Tools/equipment
- Electronics/audio
- Art/decor

Best batch size: 10 to 40 photos.

## What comes back

Each batch produces:

- master inventory table
- top valuable items
- fastest-sale items
- research queue
- bundle/donation recommendations
- first-pass prices
- marketplace listing drafts
- missing photo/detail checklist

## Decision lanes

| Lane | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Valuable | Research individually before listing |
| B | Quick local sale | List fast on Facebook Marketplace / OfferUp / Craigslist |
| C | Bundle lot | Group and sell by category or room |
| D | Exit lane | Donate, free pickup, recycle, or dispose |

## Price bands

Every item gets:

- quick sell price
- fair market price
- high ask price
- acceptable minimum

That keeps the strategy honest: move bulky stuff fast, slow down for the items that actually matter.

## Privacy rule

Before upload, photos are reviewed for:

- private documents
- addresses
- financial/medical material
- family/minor-sensitive images
- anything that should be cropped or skipped

Exact pickup address stays out of listing drafts.

## Practical first move

Start with the garage or tool area. Tools and equipment often produce the fastest useful wins: easy identification, strong local demand, and clear pricing comps.
